Labeloo support

Let's get it unstuck.

Labeloo works locally for everyday editing and printing. Accounts, project sync, and private Google Drive import are separate choices.

Import a spreadsheet

Open Labeloo, choose Import, and select Spreadsheet. You can upload Excel, Apple Numbers, LibreOffice, CSV, TSV, or text files and map their columns before adding labels. Uploaded files are parsed in your browser.

Choose a private Google Sheet

Sign in with your Wiplash.ai account, open Import, and choose Choose a Google Sheet. Google asks you to authorize Labeloo and select one spreadsheet. Labeloo receives only the file you choose through the narrow drive.file permission.

Google's verified consent screen shows the Labeloo name and logo. Review the requested file permission there before allowing access; you can cancel without changing your Labeloo project.

Import a shared Google Sheet

Expand Import a shared Google Sheet and paste the spreadsheet URL. The owner must set General access to Anyone with the link — Viewer. This path does not connect your Google account or browse your Drive.

Remove Google access

Labeloo does not retain a Google refresh token. You can also review or revoke access from your Google Account's third-party connections. Revoking access does not delete local Labeloo projects.

Printing

Print a plain-paper test first. In the browser print dialog, keep scaling at 100%, disable headers and footers, and use the paper size declared by the selected label template.

Contact Wiplash

Email support@wiplash.ai for account, import, printing, accessibility, or deletion help. Please do not attach address lists, Google authorization codes, passwords, or other sensitive spreadsheet contents.
